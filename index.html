How Raw Costing Works

Raw cost is applied only to Delivered + Shipped orders. Returned, RTO, and Cancelled orders are excluded from cost calculation since those products come back to you.

Formula: Net Profit = Net Settlement − Return Charges − (Delivered + Shipped units × Raw Cost per unit) − Ads Spend
