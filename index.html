1. What is FC Analytics? ▼
FC Analytics is a free, high-performance operational workspace built specifically for Indian marketplace sellers (Meesho, Amazon, Flipkart). It provides fast thermal label cropping, courier-wise sorting, SKU profitability calculations, and monthly payment reconciliation.
2. How does the Label Crop & Sort tool work? ▼
Simply upload your raw Meesho label PDF. The tool automatically separates multi-page labels, crops away the unnecessary lower tax invoice (or standard 4×4 trim), sorts pages by SKU or Courier Partner (Xpress Bees, Valmo, Shadowfax, Delhivery), attaches your custom messages or scannable QR codes, and generates a print-ready PDF in seconds.
3. Is my sensitive sales and customer data safe? ▼
Yes, absolutely. All PDF label processing, text extraction, Excel parsing, and calculations execute 100% locally in your browser and local database. Your files are never sent to external servers or sold to third parties.
4. Can I factor in RTO losses when calculating SKU profit? ▼
Yes! FC Analytics distinguishes between delivered sales, customer returns, and courier RTOs. It calculates the freight penalty for each returned item and computes your real blended net margin so you never scale an unprofitable product.
5. Which courier partners are recognized automatically? ▼
Our parser automatically detects and groups all major Indian e-commerce logistics partners including Xpress Bees, Valmo, Shadowfax, Delhivery, Ecom Express, Bluedart, and DTDC.
6. How does Monthly Session saving work? ▼
You can save analyzed monthly data (such as January 2026, February 2026) directly into your local database. You can revisit past sessions anytime with 1 click, compare performance trends, and track long-term growth.
7. Is FC Analytics free to use? ▼
Yes, all core shipping label sorting, cropping, profit calculators, and business analytics dashboards are completely free to use. No credit card required.

How Raw Costing Works

Raw cost is applied only to Delivered + Shipped orders. Returned, RTO, and Cancelled orders are excluded from cost calculation since those products come back to you.

Formula: Net Profit = Net Settlement − Return Charges − (Delivered + Shipped units × Raw Cost per unit) − Ads Spend
